ABOUT DETOUR

Bad choices. Bigger stories.
Another way forward.

Three short pixel-art stories about risk, friendship and finding your voice. Explore every outcome, replay decisions, and learn at your own pace. There are no timers and no perfect heroes.

i Fictional situations, satirical cartoon consequences, and no graphic injuries. Bullying is never the victim’s fault. Outcomes are possibilities, not predictions. Original chiptune music can be muted.
